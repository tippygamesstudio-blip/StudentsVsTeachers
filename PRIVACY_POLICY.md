# Privacy Policy: Students vs Teachers

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Students vs Teachers is a game for kids and families. We built it to collect nothing about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (level stars, XP, coins, unlocked gadgets) and settings (volume, view options) are saved in a file on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Playing online

When you play with friends, your device connects to theirs using a join code. The connection is only used to play the round; we do not run a server that stores anything about the players. Players cannot type or speak to each other: they can only use ready-made phrases, pings and signs, so no personal information can be shared through the game. Player names are picked from a fixed list of friendly names.

## Children

The game is made for players about 9 years old and up. Because it collects no personal information, it complies with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and GDPR (European Union). Parents are welcome to contact us with any question.

## Changes

If a future version adds online services (for example, playing with friends over the internet through a matchmaking service) or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and explain exactly what is processed and why.
